Warm architectural strip lights, discreet palm trees, wet dark stone paving with subtle golden reflections. Sophisticated automotive editorial photography, realistic materials, restrained cinematic atmosphere, near-black petroleum green and warm gold palette. Vehicle fully visible and sharp, positioned center-right, ample dark breathing space upper-left for separately rendered website text; image must also work cropped to central 4:3 on a phone. No logos, no license-plate text, no people, no text, no numbers, no border, no interface, no watermark. This is a clearly labeled demo artwork, not evidence of an actual prize. Create one scene, not a collage.
